Country Shape Matching Game

Welcome! The object of this game is to identify a country just from its shape. You can look in an atlas or on a globe to help you. If you're stumped, you can look at three clues that will help you narrow down your choices.

Even wrong answers have interesting things to tell you, so try all the countries!
