To view, and hopefully solve, a Casebook story, select one of the cases from the main Casebook page in KidsTown CityHall. Then read each page of the story. At the bottom of every page you will be presented with one or two links. Choose which of these paths you wish to follow. Once you've gathered enough clues, you'll be offered a link to Solve the Case.

Within the text of each story, clues will be revealed to assist you in solving the case. Don't be discouraged if the solution is presented before you've solved it yourself — you'll get better at finding the solutions as you read more cases.

Have Fun!
NOTE: To return to the page you were last viewing, press the "Back" button on your browser.
