KidsTown Zoo

Animals in this zoo are grouped together by where they live. Each area of the world has a unique set of animals that live there. This zoo will introduce you to some of the animals in Africa, Australia, the Oceans and the Polar Regions. Once you have read about the animals in an area, you can play a trivia game by taking the ZooKeeper's Challenge.
