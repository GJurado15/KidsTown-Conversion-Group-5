THE SUN AND THE RAIN

"Uhmmm," replied Mother Nature, "this will require the cooperation of the Rain."

"Oh, no, not the Rain!" replied the Sun. "She is the one that caused this problem in the first place."

"Oh, I think there is a misunderstanding here. Do you think that the Rain is doing this on purpose?"

"Well, yes. She wants to have fun while I have to sit doing nothing but getting bored!"

"I think that you need to be informed a little bit more on what the Rain is doing," replied Mother Nature. "Rain gives water to humans, and humans need water. Plants need water too. In fact, almost every living thing on Earth needs water."

"Well, they need me too!" replied the Sun.

"Of course they do!" affirmed Mother Nature, "but wouldn't it be nice if you and the Rain could be watching and serving the Earth at the same time?"

"Well, it seems like a good idea, but would the Rain accept?" questioned the Sun.

"I think so, but let's go ask her..."

And so both of them went to look for the Rain. When they finally found the Rain, she was sitting on a cloud. She looked unhappy.
