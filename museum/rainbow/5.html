THE SUN AND THE RAIN

"What is the problem, Rain?" asked Mother Nature.

"The Moon just visited me and told me that the Sun was upset with me because he gets bored whenever I'm giving water to the Earth," the Rain replied.

"Oh, so you know?" asked the Sun. "Well, I'm sorry for having such a wrong opinion of you. In fact, I'm here to propose something to you. I want you to help me create the 'Rain-Sun'."

"The 'Rain-Sun' - what is that?" questioned the Rain.

"I don't know yet, but Mother Nature keeps telling me that we can create something beautiful if we work together, right Mother?" replied the Sun.

"Yes," answered Mother Nature. "Well, enough talking. Do you want to help the Sun?" Mother Nature asked the Rain.

"Sure, I would gladly help him." answered the Rain.

"Let's do it!" cried the Sun.

Mother Nature said to the Rain. "I want you to water the Earth, as much as you can." To the Sun she said, "Sun, I want you to shine when the Rain is falling."

"How can I do that?" replied the Sun.

"I have given you permission to do it, so just do it." ordered Mother Nature.
